About Brew & Bean

A small cafe with a simple goal: pour great coffee and treat people well.

How we started

Brew & Bean opened in 2019 as a single espresso cart at the weekend market. Word spread fast, and within a year we had moved into our first proper shop on Coffee Lane.

Today the same care goes into every cup — we still roast in small batches and bake everything from scratch each morning.

What we stand for

  • Quality first

    Single-origin beans, roasted weekly, brewed with care in every cup.

  • Community

    A welcoming space for regulars, remote workers, and first-time visitors alike.

  • Sustainability

    Compostable cups, recycled packaging, and beans sourced from fair-trade farms.
